Wordkeep – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Contact & support: veyraxis.studio@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Wordkeep ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information on Android devices. Wordkeep is a password vault that keeps your logins on your phone, locked with words you choose.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Wordkeep has no account, no sign-in and no servers of its own. We never receive your words, your recovery words, your logins, your passwords or your notes, and we cannot see them.

2. Your Vault

Everything you save in Wordkeep (titles, usernames, passwords, websites, notes and settings) is encrypted on your phone before it is written to the App's private storage. The key is derived from your words with Argon2id, and the vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Your words and recovery words are never stored in readable form.

The vault is not sent anywhere: not to us, not to a cloud service and not to Google. Android's cloud backup is turned off for the App, so the vault is not copied to your Google account either.

Because nobody else holds a copy, nobody can recover your vault if you lose both your words and your recovery words.

3. Purchases

Wordkeep is free for up to 10 logins. Unlimited logins are a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your payment details. The App only asks Google Play whether the purchase has been made, so it can turn on unlimited logins.

4. Google Play Billing and Internet Access

The App's own code never connects to the internet. The Google Play Billing Library, which the App uses for the purchase above, includes the Android internet permission and may send technical diagnostic information about the purchase process (such as error codes and library version) to Google. This information is handled by Google, not by us, and never includes the contents of your vault.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The App shows no ads and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. It does not use the Advertising ID and does not share data with third parties.

6. Autofill and Clipboard

When you copy a username or password, it is placed on your phone's clipboard, where other apps you use may be able to read it until it is replaced. Any filling of logins into other apps happens on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Deleting Your Data

All of your Wordkeep data is stored only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  1. Open your phone's Settings and go to Apps › Wordkeep.
  2. Tap Storage › Clear storage (or Clear data), or simply uninstall Wordkeep.

This permanently deletes the encrypted vault: every login, note and setting. We keep no copy of anything, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side and nothing we could restore. A purchase of unlimited logins stays on your Google Play account and is restored when you install Wordkeep again.
